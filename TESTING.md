# Help and feedback

## Empty or outdated quest status

Zone once after loading or reloading QuestLens. It reads updates the game sends naturally. `/ql refresh` rebuilds the display; it does not fetch missing progress. Check quest data updates downloads reference information, not your character's quest log.

## A guide looks wrong or does not load

Try **Refresh guide**. BG-Wiki fallback guides may describe features outside Phoenix's era. Internet or wiki outages can leave a cached guide or local reference visible.

## An item or quest is missing

Check **Quest Items > All storage** and clear the search. Only loaded containers can be counted. Coverage is incomplete; report the item's full name and expected quest.

## Low frame rate

Compare windows closed versus a guide open. Try disabling **Quest markers** and **Debug > local quest NPC greetings** separately. Turn off marker previews and extended test range during normal play.

## Report a problem

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Nanastack/QuestLens/issues) and include:

- QuestLens and Ashita versions.
- Zone and quest/item/NPC involved.
- What you expected and what happened.
- Steps to reproduce, plus a screenshot if useful.
- For performance: approximate FPS, open windows, and enabled marker/greeting/debug options.

Do not attach your entire cache or settings folder. Review individual files before sharing them.
